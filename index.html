SpaceVital47

Oct 14–15, 2026

An astronaut health monitor prototype that turns a 1–3 minute daily self-check into a vital-monitor view of change — and switches to step-by-step emergency cards when something goes wrong, far from medical support on Earth.

HealthcareSpaceComputer VisionNASA Space Apps 2026
Live demo ↗
Details

Problem

Astronauts are not doctors, and the farther a mission goes from Earth, the harder real-time medical support becomes. Crews need a simple way to notice meaningful changes in their own health — and to know exactly what to do when it matters.

Key features

Three-layer daily checkVitals and habits (sleep, stress, resting HR, temperature, cabin CO₂) → 12 structured symptom modules with red flags → optional camera.
Vital monitorEvery channel compares today vs. yesterday vs. a personal 14-day baseline, keyed by mission day.
Respond mode13 step-by-step emergency cards with checklists, a ground-comms branch, and a copy-ready report for mission control.
Camera signalsSkin-tone change per face region and camera heart rate, with quality gates for glare, lighting, and motion.

Evidence-based design

63 conditionsMapped from NASA documentation, each tagged with how often it occurs and how reliably it can be detected.
Honest scopeEvery rule cites a source; features without evidence were dropped. The camera alone can never trigger an alert.

Build

Serverless web app with no network requests — all data stays on the device. Verified with 100 test cases (99 passing) using automated browser and synthetic camera tests.

UN SDG 11: Sustainable Community Inquiry

Jun – Aug 2024

An inquiry project to build a sustainable school community where everyone respects, supports, and grows with one another.

Presentation DesignSurvey & Research
Details
Duration
June – August 2024
Team
4 members
My role
Presentation Designer · Presenter

Execution process

  1. Chose Sustainable Community as our main theme.
  2. Discussed what makes a community sustainable and built our own definition based on respect and cooperation.
